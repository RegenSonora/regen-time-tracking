# 0038 — Regen Generalist Contractor

- **Status:** Accepted by Chris, November 17, 2026
- **Spec:** SPEC §3 (Regen General Operating, roles), §4
- **Builds on:** decisions 0027 (roles), 0029 (counts), 0030 (rates), 0034 (wording), 0035 (Certified CHW)

## Decision

1. **R-GC01 · Regen Generalist Contractor** is a new contractor role on Regen General Operating (`chw: false`).
   - No rate is prefilled. The contractor types their rate at setup.
   - No NTE (Regen General Operating has none).
2. **Budget lines and tasks** come from Chris's role map ("Regen Generalist", November 17, 2026), in the source's line order:
   - **Community Care Work** (new line `community-care-work`): Domestic support, Transportation service, Other.
   - **Resilient Home Services contracting:** the same five tasks and counts as R-TL01.
   - **Other** (`other-contractors`, as on R-TL01): Other.
   - **Leo's:** the same twelve tasks and counts as CHW-CE01, plus Flyers posted on Leo's marketing.
   - **Eat Local Network:** Forum, Full Moon Garden Party and Green Thumb Warriors event support (Event attendees), Gleaning, Food processing, Eat Local marketing, LEHR reset, Garden maintenance, LEHR maintenance, Eat Local grower support, LEHR installation, Eat Local Network: other.
3. **Seven new Regen General Operating tasks:** Gleaning, Food processing, Eat Local marketing, LEHR reset, Garden maintenance, Eat Local grower support, LEHR installation.
4. **Eight new counts:** Flyers posted, Food gleaned (lb), Food processed (lb), LEHRs reset, Soil harvested (gal), LEHR homes supported, Growers supported, LEHRs installed.
5. **LEHR reset and LEHR maintenance are separate tasks.**
   - LEHR reset counts LEHRs reset and Soil harvested.
   - LEHR maintenance counts LEHR homes supported on this role.
   - R-TL01's LEHR maintenance task keeps "LEHR resets completed".
   - How LEHR resets are paid is decision 0039.

## Decided during the build (readings of the role map)

- Two links in the map had no type. "Food processing → lbs of food processed" is read as a count, and "Eat Local Network → LEHR Installation" as a task.
- Spelling is fixed: "LEHR installation", "LEHRs reset", "Plants propagated".
- "Regen GO: Other" uses the existing contractor line Other (`other-contractors`), as on R-TL01.

## Why

Generalists pick up work across Leo's, the Eat Local Network, community care and Resilient Home Services. One role holds all of it, so each entry still bills to one budget line.
